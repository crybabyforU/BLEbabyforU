Legend for "external device":
  [HOST-BR] = built-in / USB BR/EDR Bluetooth adapter driven by BlueZ
  [BLUING]  = CSR8510-class USB dongle running Bluing firmware (BR/EDR recon)
  [ESP32]   = ESP32 board flashed with Braktooth firmware (bt_exploiter)
  [NEXUS5]  = rooted Nexus 5 phone running InternalBlue (or CYW20735 board)
  [TARGET]  = the classic Bluetooth device under test

NOTE: the lab's own numbered scripts (1-15, own_packet_full, etc.) are BLE-only.
      Everything below comes from the bundled upstream toolkits in ble-lab/tools/.
